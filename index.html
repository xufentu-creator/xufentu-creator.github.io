Research Direction

The current research examines how human judgment functions as a structural constraint in increasingly automated and AI-mediated environments, with particular attention to responsibility, governance stability, and decision clarity.

Complex Systems

Structural stability, coordination, and constraint mechanisms in high-complexity environments.

AI Governance

Governance boundaries, accountability logic, and responsibility distribution in AI-mediated systems.

Decision Architecture

Design of decision structures that preserve clarity, traceability, and meaningful human oversight.
