## Research Focus  
## 研究方向

My research develops from a continuing observation of how human judgment changes when technology, automation, replication, and system momentum become faster.

我的研究来自一个持续观察：当技术、自动化、复制与系统动量不断加快时，人类判断的位置会发生怎样的变化。

The central research line is:

**Judgment Before Momentum**  
**判断先于动量**

Human judgment is understood here as an important structural boundary between technological capability and consequential action.

在这里，人类判断被理解为技术能力与现实行动之间的重要结构边界。
